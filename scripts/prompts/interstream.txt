Flat storybook illustration with soft grain texture, gentle gradients and simple rounded shapes, in the same cosy hand-made style as a tropical-beach picture book. Square composition.

Scene: night on a calm, shallow ocean planet in deep space. A huge black hole hangs in the sky, its glowing accretion disk a bright warm ring of gold, amber and peach light bending around a perfectly black centre, with a thin halo arching over the top. The ring's light reflects as a shimmering golden path across the still water. Standing ankle-deep in the shallows in the lower third, is a small old-fashioned 1970s/80s television set: a boxy wooden cabinet on short legs, a rounded glass screen, two knobs, and a V-shaped "rabbit ears" antenna on top. The screen glows with the classic broadcast "no signal" test card (like the Philips PM5544): a big circle in the middle, bands of bright colour bars, grey and black-and-white steps and stripes like piano keys, on a grid of thin white lines; its colourful light spills softly onto the water around the TV. A small ring-shaped spaceship drifts far away near the black hole. Sky of deep indigo, navy and violet, scattered with stars, faint teal and pink nebula wisps. Mysterious, awe-filled and peaceful, about the big questions of time and space. No text, no logos, no letters.
